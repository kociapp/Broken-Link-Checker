Privacy Policy for Broken Link Checker

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Koci App ("we", "us") provides Broken Link Checker (the "App"). This policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used and shared, how long it is kept, and the choices you have.

Data collection

The App does not collect any personal data and does not use third-party services such as advertising, analytics or tracking services. Content you create in the App is not sent to us. Deleting the App removes the data it stores on your device.

Website checks

To check a site, the App requests pages directly from your device: from the website you enter and from the sites its pages link to; we do not receive the addresses or the results. A whole-site check also reads the site's robots.txt. These sites see your IP address and the App's browser identification under their own privacy policies. An address on your own network is checked only after you allow Local Network access. Checked sites and results stay on your device until you remove them from Recent or delete the App.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows when it last changed.
